Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy describes how True Health Co., Ltd. ("we") handles data in the True Health Content Report tool ("the tool"), an internal reporting tool used only by our marketing team for TikTok accounts and Facebook pages owned by our company.

1. Data we collect

When an account owner authorizes the tool with TikTok, we receive, with their permission: basic profile information (open ID, display name, username, avatar), account statistics (follower count, likes count, video count), and the list of public videos with their metadata and statistics (video ID, post time, title/description, duration, link, view, like, comment and share counts). For Facebook pages managed by our company, we read page and post statistics through the Facebook Graph API.

2. How we use the data

Only to produce internal content performance reports (videos posted per day and month, views and engagement per account and per staff member in charge). We do not use the data for advertising, profiling of individuals, or any other purpose.

3. Storage and access

Data is stored in a private Google Sheet and Google Apps Script project owned by our company. Access tokens are stored in the project's protected script properties and are never shown in the sheet. Only members of our marketing team can access the reports.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share the data with any third party.

5. Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept as long as needed for internal reporting. An account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions). When access is revoked, the tool stops collecting data, and we delete the stored data for that account on request.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Marketing Department of True Health Co., Ltd.
